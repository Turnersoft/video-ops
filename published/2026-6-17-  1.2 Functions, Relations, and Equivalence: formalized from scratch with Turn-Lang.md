### Functions and well-defined (2:58–4:05)

A function is not a separate species. It is a relation that behaves like a machine. You feed in a, you get exactly one b, and the pair [a,b] is in this relation. Turn-Lang puts the uniqueness check on Relation as the well_defined property. A function is literally just a relation which is well defined. Instead of writing pairs by hand we write f(a). The book shows a classical counterexample which is not a function. This is what not well-defined means in plain language — one input but there are multiple output, the mapping is not unique. Our well_defined property formalizes exactly that: for each a, exist a unique b with the pair of a and b in the relation.
